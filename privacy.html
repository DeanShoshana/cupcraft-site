Privacy Policy

Updated November 12, 2026

Privacy by design

CupCraft is a local-first app. It does not require an account and the developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share personal data through the app.

Information stored on your device

Beans, brew logs, espresso shot logs, tasting notes, recipes, maintenance routines, timer history, ratings, notes, and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device.

When you choose a photo, CupCraft copies it into the app's private storage for journal use.

iCloud sync

Versions that include iCloud sync store your journal, photos, timer history, and maintenance records in your private iCloud database so you can access them on CupCraft devices signed into the same Apple Account. Apple operates iCloud under its privacy policy. The developer does not receive or access your private iCloud records. CupCraft can save records on your device when iCloud is unavailable. Device preferences and exports stay on the device unless you choose to share an export.

Notifications

If you enable maintenance reminders, CupCraft schedules local notifications on your device. Notification permission and delivery are managed by iOS or iPadOS.

Exports and sharing

CupCraft can create JSON, CSV, and PDF exports on your device. Nothing is shared unless you intentionally choose a destination through Apple's share sheet. Once exported, the copy is controlled by the destination you select.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

CupCraft contains no third-party analytics, advertising SDK, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated telemetry. Apple may provide diagnostics under the settings and policies of your Apple account and device.

Data retention and deletion

Use CupCraft’s deletion controls to remove journal records, photos, and maintenance history. If you use iCloud sync, these deletions apply across devices on the same Apple Account after they sync. Removing the app from one device removes its local copy. Synced records may remain in your private iCloud account. The developer has no separate copy or CupCraft account to delete.

Children

CupCraft is a general-audience coffee journaling and educational app that is not directed specifically to children; the developer does not knowingly receive personal information from children through the app.

Changes

If CupCraft's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed behavior is released.
